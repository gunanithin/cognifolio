Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does Cognifolio analyse PDF documents with AI?

Cognifolio uses enterprise-grade vision and language models combined with high-precision OCR to parse text, tabular structures, and layout coordinates. When you ask a question or request a summary, answers are directly tied to highlighted bounding boxes on the actual document pages.

Is it free to analyse PDFs on Cognifolio?

Yes. Cognifolio is 100% free during its public beta. You can upload documents, chat with PDFs, extract tables, and compare contracts without a subscription or credit card.

Can I analyse financial spreadsheets and balance sheets?

Yes. Cognifolio includes a built-in financial spreadsheet data grid that allows sheet-tab switching, row numbering, sticky headers, and instant row filtering alongside AI analysis.

Is my uploaded PDF data private and secure?

Yes. Cognifolio enforces zero-trust data isolation. PII is masked before model processing, and your files are strictly siloed to your session with zero cross-tenant leakage.
